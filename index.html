MORE THAN A SPICE TRADE

A coast that has
always welcomed the world.

For centuries, ships came to Kozhikode for its pepper. We carry that story forward, connecting the growers of the Western Ghats with kitchens, makers and markets across the globe.

Malabar Crown began with a simple belief: good trade starts with knowing where things come from, and who made them possible. From our home in Kozhikode, we work closely with farming families, processors and buyers to make every shipment one we can stand behind.
